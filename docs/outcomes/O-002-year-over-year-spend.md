# O-002 — Year-over-year spend

**Outcome** — Enable client portal users to see their spend with craftlions per year — grouped by project, back to the start of the relationship — so that budget planning, invoice reconciliation, and value review are self-served in the portal instead of hand-summed from invoices or waited on for days.

**Success**

- Client comparing years hand-sums invoices: every time → never; the dashboard states each year's total.
- Client reconciling their books waits days for our numbers: happens → self-served the moment they look.
- A past year's spend is a number with no story: today → every year shows which projects it funded, rest on one unassigned line.

# O-003 — Traceable invoice context

**Outcome** — Enable invoice reviewers to move from an invoice to its exact project, phase, and delivery evidence without comparing wording or searching across separate places, so that they can verify the context and pay on time.

**Success**

- Finding an invoice’s project and phase by matching wording across pages: required today → reached directly from the invoice.
- Searching elsewhere for the delivered work behind an invoice: required today → the relevant delivery PR or MR opens directly from the invoice and its exact phase.
- Manual context gathering contributes to late payments: recurring → rare.
